CATIA V5 Macros

Ten small automations for the most repeated CAD tasks

A macro is the lightest possible tool: a single CATVBS file that runs inside CATIA, needs no installation and does one job well. I wrote these for tasks colleagues repeated dozens of times a day, and for a few one-off jobs where a deadline made manual work impossible.

How They Are Built

Same rules for every macro.

Every macro carries its own header: description, requirements, step-by-step usage, warnings, author and license. Macros that change the document never save automatically and stop with a clear message when the input is not what they expect. Each one is distributed as a numbered pair of files, an executable .catvbs and an identical .txt for reading.
