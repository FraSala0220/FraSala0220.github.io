Featured · PB Weight

Composite weight estimation: from 12 hours to about one minute per ply-book.

12 h → 1 min Per ply-book, manual versus automated
~720× Faster (99.9% time reduction)
10+ Ply-books validated with correct results
~2 Ply-books to pay back 20 h of development

Problem. A weight estimate meant reading every ply step of the ply-book in CATIA, measuring its surfaces and copying areas, materials and ply counts into a weight calculation and a weight report. About 12 hours per ply-book, with real risk of a missed or double-counted ply.

Structure. A CATVBS extractor walks the CATIA tree top-down and exports every step. A JScript bridge, run by Windows Script Host, recognizes the two Excel workbooks by their internal structure and fills them through COM, keeping template formulas and formatting. Only CATIA, Excel and Windows are needed: no Python, PowerShell, VBA or admin rights.

Rules and checks. Multipliers follow one explicit priority and never multiply each other. Every tree node is logged as exported, warning, container, excluded or error, and nothing unreconciled is hidden. Workbooks are edited as copies and replaced only after validation.

Lesson. Ply-books were structured differently by different engineers. We agreed on a standard structure instead of adding special cases, which made the tool simpler and more reliable. Co-developed with the engineer who requested it.

The Other Four Tools

Structure and effectiveness, tool by tool.

Drawing Notes Manager · Pilot

Standard notes on drawings

Structure

  • CATVBS engine plus a small HTA picker window.
  • Notes in a plain-text library, layout in a config file.
  • Runs on locked-down PCs by copying the folder.

Effectiveness

  • 2 min → ≤1 min per drawing (≥50%).
  • Fewer forgotten or outdated notes; ~10 drawings validated.

Airfoil Mesh · In use

STEP to surface mesh for airfoils

Structure

  • Two Python modules on Gmsh: triangles (STL) and true quads (MSH, VTU).
  • Drag a STEP file onto a .bat; guided, uniform or extruded-profile modes.
  • Fails loudly if a "full quad" mesh still contains triangles.

Effectiveness

  • Quality comparable to ANSA on tested airfoils, in seconds.
  • Used daily by two meshers; saves license use, not time.

Fast CAD Viewer · Pilot

STEP assemblies without a CAD seat

Structure

  • Python, PySide6 and OpenCascade, packaged as a portable folder.
  • Part tree, fast hide/show, section planes and search.

Effectiveness

  • No CATIA license needed just to look at an assembly.
  • Tested on company PCs, no access rights required.

DXF Mirror LH → RH · Released

Right-hand templates from left-hand ones

Structure

  • One Python script with an embedded runtime and a .bat launcher.
  • Edits DXF text directly: only geometry changes, layers and handles stay.

Effectiveness

  • A whole folder of LH templates mirrored in one run.
  • Texts stay readable; fiber-direction symbols are not flipped.
